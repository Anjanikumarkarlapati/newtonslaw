🚀 Newton's Laws Playground

Physics is the rulebook for how the universe moves!

1st Law: The Law of Laziness (Inertia) 😴

What is it? Objects are "lazy." If an object is sitting still, it wants to stay sitting still. If it is moving, it wants to keep moving in a straight line forever!

So why do things stop? Because of invisible forces like Friction (rubbing against the ground) and Air Resistance (pushing through the air).

🧠 Brain Bite: When you ride in a car and it suddenly stops, your body jerks forward. That's your body's inertia wanting to keep moving forward at the speed the car was just going!

2nd Law: Force = Mass x Acceleration 💪

What is it? This law is a math equation! It means the heavier something is (Mass), the harder you have to push it (Force) to make it speed up (Acceleration).

The Golden Rule: Big heavy things are hard to move. Small light things are easy to move!

🧠 Brain Bite: Imagine kicking a beach ball as hard as you can. It flies super fast! Now imagine kicking a bowling ball with that exact same force. Ouch! It barely moves because it has much more mass.

3rd Law: Action & Reaction 💥

What is it? Forces always come in pairs! You cannot touch something without it touching you back. For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

🧠 Brain Bite: How do birds fly? Their wings push the air DOWN (Action). Because of the 3rd law, the air pushes the bird UP (Reaction)! Rockets work the same way in space by pushing fire out the back.
